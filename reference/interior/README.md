# Interior reference photos

Drop photos of the FL5 interior in this folder. They are used as reference for
rebuilding the cockpit, and some may become textures.

The current cockpit is a simple stand-in built in code.

## Most useful shots

Take them in daylight, parked, in landscape orientation.

1. **Driver's eye view.** Seated normally, phone at eye height, looking
   straight ahead through the windscreen. This sets every proportion.
2. **Instrument cluster, straight on.** Ignition on. One in Normal or Sport
   mode and one in +R mode. Fill the frame.
3. **Steering wheel, straight on.** Wheel centred.
4. **Whole dashboard.** From the middle of the back seat, wide enough to show
   both door mirrors.
5. **Centre console and gear lever.** From above and from the driver's seat.
6. **Left and right A-pillars and door tops.** From the driver's eye position,
   looking toward each mirror.
7. **Shift lights.** Any shot showing where they sit on the cluster hood.

## Before sharing

This project is public. Check that no photo shows a licence plate, a VIN plate,
registration papers, a garage door opener, a house or anything else that
identifies you.

This folder is listed in `.gitignore`, so photos stay on your machine unless
you choose to publish them.
